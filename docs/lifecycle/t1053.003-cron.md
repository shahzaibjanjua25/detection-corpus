Promotion path: `experimental` → `test` after two weeks of shadow mode,
target < 10 FPs/day per 1,000 hosts. The package-manager filter is the
likely source of residual noise on Debian/Ubuntu fleets (dpkg maintainer

`ppid` allow-list for `dpkg`/`apt` parent processes if FP rate is high.
→ `stable` once the config-management and package-manager filters are
validated against the fleet's actual tooling paths and FP rate is < 2/day.
