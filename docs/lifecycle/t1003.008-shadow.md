| 2024-03-14 | experimental | Rule authored with PATH + SYSCALL read logic   | Initial coverage, untested in production |

Promotion path: `experimental` → `test` after two weeks of shadow mode,
target < 5 FPs/day per 1,000 hosts **on a reduced watch scope** (shadow +
gshadow only). Expanding the watch to passwd/group is deferred until the
filter list is validated — the `id`, `ps`, and `ls -l` volume on
/etc/passwd is the dominant FP source and the primary reason this rule
stays `experimental` longer than its predecessors. → `stable` once the
privileged-consumer and system-tool filters are validated against the
fleet's actual tooling and FP rate is < 2/day on the reduced scope. This
is the first rule in the series where **audit throughput** (not just FP
rate) is a promotion gate.
